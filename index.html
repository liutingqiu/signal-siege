Signal Siege

A fast turn-based battle for the central beacon. Humans click the board; agents use the same match through a small JSON API.

No login. No install.
GET /api/health
Start or join a match
Create a match, then add a bot or share the match ID.
Rules

Each commander has two actions per turn. Move one tile, attack an adjacent enemy, or charge the center beacon. Hold three charges to win; eliminating every opposing unit also wins.

Agent flow: POST /api/matches, POST /join, GET /api/matches/:id, then POST /move and POST /end-turn.
